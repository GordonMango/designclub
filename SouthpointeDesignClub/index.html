About Design Club

デザインクラブについて

Founded in 2026, Design Club is a year-round club where students receive the opportunity to innovate almost anything.

The goal of this club is to equip students from grades 5–12 with the skills and connections to succeed in the field of STEM — a field where there is a real need for adaptability and experience.

These initiatives drive our mission of lifelong learning and global academic excellence.

Why STEM competitions matter

なぜ競技会か
01

STEM competitions are rare

Most STEM competitions require lots of funding and a large student body — the Conrad Challenge, for example, requires more than 10 members. As a student collective, we have a huge advantage: funding from collaborative fundraising and the necessary resources in order to compete.

02

Innovation is defining the decade

In a world revolving around artificial intelligence and automation, STEM is becoming an increasingly vital domain. Understanding the implications of STEM can open many doors to high-paying opportunities and job security in today’s status quo.

Principal investigators

主任研究員

What is novelty

The best innovations are unique and original — which is what makes them novel.

Why make novelty

Novelty is what makes innovations useful. Imagine a million copies of a single innovation — it doesn’t change anything. A part of this club is to make something useful, but more importantly to make a change in the world. Consequently, we have implemented a PI system where our mentors and experts will support students in ensuring that their projects encompass that unique spark.

Who are the experts

We will do our best to contact experts in many disciplines, such as artificial intelligence, to join our hosted webinars. It is up to students to attend meetings and ask questions that might benefit their project.

At the same time, we acknowledge that this involves a relative amount of trust between students. That is why we will hold 1:1 meetings with students during appropriate times, and ensure that projects between students do not overlap as much as possible.

Leadership & opportunities

リーダーシップ

Design Club is building a student-led team dedicated to creating accessible opportunities in engineering, research, innovation, and competition. Applications for leadership, mentor, and investigator positions will be opening soon.

Vice President / Operations Lead

Seniors

Supports the President and manages the club’s day-to-day operations, meetings, project timelines, member coordination, and internal organization.

› Expected to attend and help direct meetings unless stated otherwise.

Director of Competitions

Seniors

Researches competition opportunities, manages registration and deadlines, organizes competition teams, and helps members prepare for events such as Skills Canada, science fairs, and engineering challenges.

› Expected to find an opportunity every 2 months and provide a general overview for students.

Secretariat of Outreach

Seniors

Builds relationships with universities, engineering organizations, companies, researchers, and community partners. Responsible for contacting organizations, professors and PhD researchers to develop mentorships, workshops, guest-speaker opportunities, and partnerships.

› Expected to host a webinar at least once every 3 months. Suggested to collaborate with the Director of Competitions.

Director of Sponsorship & Fundraising

Seniors

Leads sponsorship outreach and fundraising initiatives to support equipment, competition fees, engineering projects, and the club’s future robotics program.

› Expected to host hardware collection initiatives for the Electronic Recycling Association and collaborate with the Secretariat of Outreach.

Social Media Managers

All ages openings

Manages the club’s social media presence and creates regular posts highlighting projects, competitions, workshops, achievements, recruitment opportunities, and upcoming events. Multiple positions are open and highly recommended.

› Expected 1 post every 1.5 weeks. Needs to be professional and somewhat informative.

Engineering Mentors

All ages openings

Multiple mentor positions will be available for students with experience in specific technical areas.

CAD & Mechanical Design Mentors

All ages openings

Teaches members CAD, 3D modelling, mechanical design, prototyping, and engineering design principles. Multiple openings are available for students who can support beginners.

Coding & AI Mentors

All ages openings

Supports members working on websites, Python, JavaScript, Arduino logic, AI experiments, debugging, and interactive project demos.

Electronics & Robotics Mentors

All ages openings

Helps teams with circuits, sensors, motors, microcontrollers, wiring, testing, and robotics troubleshooting during build sessions.

Research & Pitch Writers

All ages openings

Writes project summaries, competition pitches, research notes, grant blurbs, and clear documentation that helps technical ideas make sense.

Prototype & Fabrication Assistants

All ages openings

Supports hands-on building, material prep, simple fabrication, testing logs, iteration notes, and cleanup after prototype sessions.

Event & Workshop Assistants

All ages openings

Helps set up workshops, welcome members, organize supplies, track attendance, photograph progress, and keep sessions running smoothly.

Some considerations. If you do not find any role that interests you, feel free to talk to Gordon about new role openings that might suit your interest. As a design club, we appreciate any form of innovation — including your own ideal leadership roles.

Inconsistent participation in meetings, violation of rules, misconduct, and disrespecting members may result in leadership roles being removed.

No single type of experience defines a strong applicant. We are looking for leaders who are curious and willing to learn.

/ Strategic plan

Three pillars to success

三つの柱
01

Design and redefine

Members begin by developing original ideas and transforming them into practical designs, prototypes, or engineering solutions. Through feedback from peers, mentors, and industry professionals, students strengthen their technical skills, improve their work, and learn how to approach challenges through an iterative design process.

02

Compete and achieve

Members apply their knowledge and skills through engineering, science, and design competitions. Competition provides students with opportunities to work toward clear objectives, solve problems under real-world constraints, collaborate effectively, and demonstrate their abilities beyond the classroom.

03

Lead and contribute

Members are encouraged to take leadership roles within the club and the broader STEM community. Through mentoring, project management, outreach, and team leadership, students develop the ability to support others, organize meaningful initiatives, and create opportunities for future members to succeed.

Partnerships — As a club starting off, we understand that starting an organization alone is difficult. We do not have the funding necessary, the newest technology, or access to many competitions. Hence, we have decided to collaborate with organizations that support diverse student bodies and mentors with experienced backgrounds to teach us the skills necessary. Thank you to Harvard Student Agencies and the organizations that have sponsored and supported us.
